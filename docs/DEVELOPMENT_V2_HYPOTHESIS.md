# Prospective frozen Development Suite 2 hypothesis

Written before pilot outcomes and final strategy comparisons. The hypothesis is that unchanged V0.3 counterexample guidance improves architecture search on structurally diverse, independently screened tasks with reachable distance headroom. This is a conditional finite-development-suite test, not a random sample of all stabilizer codes.

Admission is governed solely by DEVELOPMENT_V2_SELECTION_PROTOCOL.md. No V0.3 pilot results enter selection. The manifest and all code hashes will be committed before any final comparison. Ten final seeds 10–19, six shared initial architectures, 64 evaluation slots, target per task, max weight three, work/spectrum caps 200000, R=3, ideal input, physical product readout, logical index zero and no implicit idles. Every other algorithm parameter remains exactly frozen. All five primary strategies run all seeds. Previously frozen ablations use seeds 10–12 as secondary descriptive checks, with no promotion or tuning.

Four confirmatory endpoints, each V0.3 versus random/resource/V0.2 (12 comparisons):

1. Target attained at least once within the fixed budget (higher better).
2. First evaluation reaching target, unreached assigned 65 (lower better). This penalized endpoint is not an estimate of uncensored completion time.
3. Strongest rigorously certified distance lower bound (higher better); unknown upper bounds are not distances.
4. Minimum QD at the highest tier jointly attained by both paired runs, requiring tier>=2 (lower better). Missing matched tiers are excluded and denominator/missingness reported. Resource comparisons do not silently assign zero to failures.

For each comparison, first average paired oriented differences over tasks within each seed. Apply an exact two-sided sign-flip permutation over the ten seed blocks; all code/basis observations sharing a seed flip together. This prevents pretending code/basis repetitions or ancestry edges are independent trials. Holm family-wise correction across all twelve tests at alpha=.05. W/T/L per task/seed and per code are descriptive, not independent-binomial evidence. Bootstrap percentile 95% effect intervals resample seeds and construction families (2000 draws, seed 20261004); few families impose a major limitation. Include a family-block sign-flip sensitivity test. Do not interpret conditional seed evidence as arbitrary-code generalization.

Sign flipping assumes exchangeable/symmetric paired seed-block differences under the null; strategies themselves were not randomly assigned labels. The p values are conditional model-based evidence, not a distribution-free causal randomized trial. Final seeds are deterministic prospective samples, and few construction families remain a separate generalization limitation.

Confirmatory endpoints use bounds attained by the unchanged frozen search/certification engine. Independent post-run auditing may additionally resolve capped initial architectures or proxy circuits to a stronger physical bound; record this as a separate diagnostic and never feed it into search or silently replace the confirmatory endpoint. Audit final-seed initial target quality to reveal residual difficulty/calibration censoring; do not reselect tasks after seeing those seeds. Independence-based proxy certificates may establish the runtime gate when total search-plus-audit time meets its threshold, while native-engine censoring remains an explicit limitation.

A positive development advantage must pass at least one confirmatory endpoint against EACH required baseline, in the favorable direction with Holm p<=.05, and have a practical margin: target success >=10 percentage points, penalized evaluations >=8 slots, distance >=.1 tier, or matched-tier QD >=10% improvement. Favorable results must span at least three distinct non-control code instances, at least two construction families, and at least six final seeds. A non-CSS generator control shares its parent; direct sums are labeled and do not count as independent original families. Overlap of bases is not additional family replication.

Secondary mechanism endpoints: complete comparable frontier-class reductions, exposure-adjusted association of neutral reductions with later descendant distance gain, and strict contiguous neutral reducing steps followed by a certified gain. Report all arms, denominators, parent links, operators, M_t,d_c and Q/D/QD changes. 'Requiring composition' cannot be inferred from observational ancestry; measure observed composition only. Do not run significance tests on search edges as independent trials. Exploration of class counts, check weights/overlaps, mechanism diversity, k, CSS representation and headroom is explicitly post-hoc descriptive.

Any all-strategy failure is classified using independently certified pilot witnesses and resource/certification limits: a reachable witness establishes search-budget/algorithm failure, not impossibility. No bounded failed pilot proves unreachability. Unresolved boundary analysis is ineligible. All claimed gains require independent new-circuit exclusion through lower_bound-1, replay of upper witnesses, and honest source-class transport status. Global exclusion eliminates all valid lower-tier classes, even if exact parent-to-child fault-address transport is ambiguous.

Runtime is reported both at equal evaluation budget and at equal elapsed-time checkpoints (25%,50%,100% of the median random arm time per task); full analysis overhead is included. Certification time, examined combinations, candidates/sec and process peak memory are measured. Unrelated development proxies near 23/25/31/44 qubits may be used for scaling only, never held-out inputs. A practical smaller-size estimate means at least one unrelated n>=20 proxy completes 32 slots with exact lower-tier certification in <=1800 seconds and peak working set <=2 GiB. Larger-size projections are estimates, not certified practicality.

READY_FOR_HELD_OUT requires passing tests, prospectively frozen selection, multiple genuine-headroom tasks, the reproducible advantage above, independent certification, the runtime gate, and zero held-out information use. Otherwise NOT_READY_FOR_HELD_OUT; diagnose benchmark, algorithm, compute or fault-model limitations. Do not weaken criteria or change the algorithm after results. HELD_OUT_FREEZE.md is created only if all gates pass, and no held-out codes run here.

## Pre-comparison compute-plan amendment

Before any final strategy result, measured runtime of the unrelated n25 proxy was 242.76 seconds for 32 slots, dominated by 212.98 seconds of spectrum analysis. The complete suite test run under concurrent screening/proxies took 225 seconds versus 30 seconds before this workload. To avoid excessive compute, select at most eight tasks in the previously specified family/code round-robin order and use the original frozen 32-slot budget for all final primary and secondary arms. Keep ten final seeds, all five primary arms, all five frozen secondary ablations on three seeds, targets and every statistical/practical/spread threshold unchanged. The failed-to-reach penalty is therefore 33, not 65. Pilots retain 64 slots. This is a prospectively documented compute allocation, not algorithm tuning or a change prompted by comparative strategy performance. Final manifest and its committed hash are authoritative. Preserve the original proposed 64-slot plan above to make this amendment visible.

Runtime-gate clarification before final comparison: the smaller-size proxy must independently exclude through weight two (best lower bound >=3), in addition to the originally stated 32-slot/time/memory conditions. Exclusion of singleton faults alone is insufficient to establish practical two-fault certification. Preserve and report capped timings, but do not declare deeper certification practical from them.
